# The "Reframed" Career Narrative: From Support to Sustaining Engineering

> **Goal:** Position yourself as a "Stability Expert" and "Sustaining Engineer" who solves high-impact problems, rather than a support agent who just closes tickets.

## Part 1: The "Tell Me About Yourself" Script

**The Strategy:**
1.  **Lead with Engineering:** Establish yourself immediately as a developer with 5+ years of expertise.
2.  **Rebrand "Support":** Call it "Sustaining Engineering," "Production Reliability," or "Core Stability."
3.  **Focus on Impact:** Talk about *what* you fixed (performance, race conditions, memory leaks), not *how* you received the work (tickets).

### The Script

"I am a Senior Software Engineer with over 5 years of experience specializing in the MEAN stack, particularly deep Angular architectures and Node.js backend systems.

For the past 3 years, I’ve been acting as a **Lead Sustaining Engineer** for a critical production application. In this role, I wasn't just building features; I was responsible for the **architectural stability and health** of a massive codebase. My focus has been on three key areas:

1.  **Complex Root Cause Analysis:** dissecting high-priority production incidents that junior developers couldn't crack—things like obscure memory leaks in Angular switching between modules, or race conditions in our Node.js API layer under high load.
2.  **Refactoring & Technical Debt Reduction:** Transforming legacy code into modern, maintainable patterns. For example, I led the initiative to migrate our older Promise-based chains to clean `async/await` flows and introduced strict typing interfaces where they were missing.
3.  **Performance Optimization:** I worked deep in the optimize cycle, improving our application's initial load time by restructuring our lazy-loading strategy and fine-tuning our Change Detection strategies to eliminate unnecessary usage of the digest cycle.

Currently, I’m looking for a Core Development role where I can apply this deep knowledge of 'what breaks applications' to 'building bulletproof applications from day one'."
